About ZAC

Making AI and automation accessible to Malaysian SMEs.

ZAC AI Technologies is a Petaling Jaya company serving SMEs across Malaysia and Southeast Asia, through three divisions: Edge, Software and AI Lab.

Vision

To be Malaysia’s pioneering force in democratising advanced AI, empowering SMEs with new-generation data intelligence, automation and machine learning.

Mission

To bridge the gap between innovation and accessibility, with data-driven solutions that help businesses operate smarter, decide faster and grow sustainably.

Through machine learning, fintech analytics and AI engineering, we turn complex data into actionable insight for a connected, future-ready Malaysian economy.

Our team

Globally trained. Building what’s next for Malaysia.

An all-star team of globally trained professionals, Malaysian graduates from different countries, bringing diverse perspectives and strong professional skills.

  1. Consulting team

    Analyses business workflows, finds the AI opportunities and designs tailored integration roadmaps.

  2. Engineering team

    API integration, machine learning model customisation and system architecture, built for real-world use.

  3. Operations team
