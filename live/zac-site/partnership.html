Partnering with ZAC

Bring ZAC in where your clients need AI.

ZAC helps SMEs across Malaysia and Southeast Asia adopt AI and automation. A partner is a firm that already works with SMEs and brings ZAC in when a client needs it, across our three divisions.

  1. ZAC Edge · Consulting & strategy

    Workflow analysis, AI opportunities and tailored integration roadmaps.

  2. ZAC Software · Custom engineering

    Custom systems, API integration and system architecture.

  3. ZAC AI Lab · Applied AI

    Machine learning model customisation and applied AI on a client’s own data.
